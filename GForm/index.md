## 🎯 Share Your Interview Experience 🎯

Help future candidates by sharing your interview journey! Your experience can make a real difference.

---

## 👨‍🍳 What We're Looking For

- **Company name** and position you interviewed for
- **Interview format** (phone, video, in-person, or hybrid)
- **Interview rounds** and duration
- **Technical questions** asked (coding, system design, etc.)
- **Behavioral questions** and cultural fit assessment
- **Your preparation strategy** and resources used
- **Tips and advice** for future candidates
- **Overall experience** and outcome

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

::: tip Is my submission anonymous?
You can choose to submit anonymously or include your name/contact information – it's completely up to you!
:::

::: info How long does it take?
Most people complete the form in 5-10 minutes. Take your time to provide detailed information.
:::

::: warning What should I avoid sharing?
Please don't share any confidential information, proprietary code, or NDA-protected content. Keep it general and helpful!
:::

---

## 🧠 Why Share Your Experience?

Your insights help thousands of job seekers prepare better and approach their interviews with confidence. Every detail matters – from the questions asked to the overall atmosphere.

---

## 🫲 Guidelines for Quality Submissions

### ✅ Do Include:

- Specific questions asked (without NDA violations)
- Interview structure and timeline
- Preparation resources that helped
- Tips for future candidates
- Honest assessment of difficulty

### ❌ Avoid:

- Confidential or proprietary information
- Exact coding solutions to problems
- Negative comments about interviewers personally
- Information covered by NDAs
